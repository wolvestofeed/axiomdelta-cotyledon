## Steps

- [x] (S1) Subscriptions (migration `0019`; `src/data/subscriptions.ts`, `src/engine/subscriptions.ts`, `src/engine/subscription-cutoffs.ts`, `src/server/subscription-actions.ts`, `src/components/SubscriptionsSection.tsx`; `test/farm-subscriptions.test.ts`), as agreed with Rob: a subscription is a subscriber's standing order at one pickup point, weekly, every two weeks, or monthly on the same weekday of the same week of the month (a monthly one starts in the first four weeks), from its first distribution, with an optional last date and a flat plan of grow plans and units kept as dated versions. A distribution carries the version in force on its date, nothing when skipped, paused or on a farm closure. Every rule reads the distribution's sow date, the earliest of its lines' (`sowDateFor`): a subscription starts on a first distribution that can still be sown for, a distribution is skipped or unskipped only before its sow date, a pause and a flat plan change start at the first distribution not yet sown, a resume turns the distributions missed while paused into skips, and a last date cannot fall before a distribution already sown. A line's plan is in service and offered on the subscriber's channel. The order book derives one forecast order per line on each distribution carried, source Subscription, at the subscriber's price else the channel's, and a stored order naming the subscription (`orders.subscription_id`) replaces it; the Orders page names the subscription. Subscribers shows each subscriber's subscriptions with the next five distributions, their sow dates and what each carries and costs, and the edits the sow dates allow, on Actual, super admins; the Subscriber Portal gets them once accounts are linked
- [x] (S1a) Dark and lit racks, as Rob grows (migration `0020`): a grow unit marked dark stages only (Grow Units, the fixture select) holds a tray sowing through its sow day, germination and blackout, stacked five to a shelf place through the sow day and germination (`GERMINATION_STACK`, STATED) and spread in blackout; the shelf ledger places a sowing's dark days on a dark rack and its light days on a lit unit, else its whole cycle on a lit unit (`ShelfLedger.place`, `darkPlacesPerTray`); the calendar counts a dark rack's shelf places; the ceiling is the lesser of the lit trays over the light days and the dark places over the place-days a tray takes there (`deriveGrowCapacity`, the Capacity chain). The home seed is two of Vallecito's lit racks with ten VG80s and two dark racks of the same shelving at five tiers, synced to the local workspace; a weekly sowing of 20 is placed every week with all four racks holding trays.
