**Where the work stands.** A subscription is a subscriber's standing order at one pickup point: weekly, every two weeks, or monthly on the same weekday of the same week, with a flat plan of grow plans and units kept as dated versions; skips, pauses, flat plan changes and last dates are held to the distribution's sow date. Demand and the order book are the subscriptions alone: the service model (daily services, volume picks, the service calendar, saved cycles, flat plans by rotation, participation, the subscriber kind, enrollment and the school-era tray formats) is out of the schema (migration `0023`), the engine, the server, the pages and the tests. A sowing is the orders flowing into production: the whole trays a variety's orders need, one flat the least, sown on the day its days to harvest end on the Saturday distribution (sprouts on a production day), split across units only past what one holds. The next run on Procurement and the Suppliers page is the next distribution date with an order, each plan sown on its own sow date. A finished lot is stock for its plan's harvest window and waste after it; there is no shelf-life figure. A unit is a tray on every channel. Light is costed at the shelf's fixtures at full power; the shrink allowance grosses up seed, medium and nutrient only; a purchase order closes only when received in full. The plan of record is written on its first apply in any workspace; the Actual ledger is costed at the plan of record, never from the browser's working copy. The home grow room is two lit racks and two dark racks; any lit rack takes any plan. The seed is nineteen Forecast Subscribers (Plan only, invented) and Own use (Rob), each one tray weekly at the Saturday pickup from 17 October 2026: 20 a week; Actual carries only Rob's own tray, which goes to Owner Draws at cost. A flat is $30 (Subscriptions stated; Restaurants and Retail and wholesale a placeholder). The opening equity, the loan date, the payroll calendar, the shrink allowance and the closedown are PLACEHOLDER figures carried from the source until Rob states them.
