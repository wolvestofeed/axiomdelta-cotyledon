## 3. Decisions to agree with Rob

Each is written as the plan proposes it. None is adopted until Rob agrees.

1. **Two functional units, both always shown.** The tray in its format is the operational unit: it is what is sown, costed, sold and returned, and every engine figure is per tray. The kilogram of fresh harvested microgreens is the comparable unit: it is what the literature reports and what the "acre-feet and fuels" comparison divides by. Per kg is per tray divided by the plan's harvest grams; while harvest grams are PLACEHOLDER the per-kg figure carries that tag and the per-tray figure does not. A sprout in a jar is per jar and per kg the same way.
2. **No live LCA engine.** Factors stay versioned static reference data in `emission-factors.ts`, each with publisher, URL, version, effective date and tag, as design rule 1 of the Muse scope. Ecoinvent is out unless the center buys a licence. Agribalyse and the USDA Federal LCA Commons are the public background databases; a dataset drawn from either is registered as a document with the process name as the figure's locator. OpenLCA, if Rob installs it, is where he builds a process; its result enters the app as a registered figure with the model file attached, exactly as a supplier's LCA does today.
3. **The boundary is cradle to hand-off, plus the end of life of what the farm throws away.** Upstream: seed, medium, nutrient, packaging and tray wear, with their freight to the grow room. Use: light, climate, water, and the share of the household's or the facility's energy the grow room takes. Downstream: the mat and roots, unsold trays, packaging, by pathway. A subscriber's own transport to a pickup point, and the tray's return trip, are outside the boundary until Pickup Points and Routes exist (Phase 3); the app says so on the page.
4. **Capital goods are amortized over a stated service life, per tray-day of use.** A rack, a fixture, a fan and a tray set carry a mass, a material and a service life as open fields on Equipment, the way footprints and sowing capacity already do; the engine spreads the embodied CO2e over the tray-days the unit serves in the year. The tray set already amortizes its dollars over 1,000 uses (`tray-formats.ts`); its CO2e amortizes the same way. The 50-year life the document cites for steel is UNCONFIRMED and is not the default; the default is null, shown as not stated.
5. **Global warming potential is the inventory; eutrophication and water are carried per input where the factor has them; acidification waits.** The ledger posts CO2e. The Poore & Nemecek rows already carry eutrophication (kg PO4-eq/kg) and freshwater withdrawal (L/kg), and a seed or nutrient factor from Agribalyse carries them too; the Inputs page shows them per tray beside the CO2e, never summed with it. Acidification enters when a factor set the app registers carries it.
6. **No eutrophication credit for containment, and no biogenic carbon credit for the hemp.** The document argues that bottom watering in flats brings runoff to near zero and that hemp sequesters carbon while it grows. Neither is claimed. Discharge is what the water page records (a sprout jar's spent rinse water is a discharge; a tray's bottom water that the mat holds is not); the hemp mat's factor is its production and transport, with its composting shown as the end-of-life pathway on the Waste page, and the carbon stored in the fiber is noted, not netted.
7. **Energy on Plan is derived, not typed.** The forecast knows every tray-day under light (`grow-calendar.ts` `underLight`), the shelf's fixtures and their watts (`capex.ts` `shelfLights`, `inputs-catalog.ts` `LIGHT_FIXTURES`) and the regime's photoperiod; the kWh the lights draw is arithmetic over that, and it is the kWh the light line is already costed at (`lightCostPerTrayDay`). The typed annual kWh remains for what the calendar cannot see (fans, climate, the rest of the building), and on Actual the bill stands over both, with the grow room's share allocated the way the household water already is.
8. **The light regime's energy is a fact beside its nutrition, never a verdict.** The document's trade-off between phytonutrient elicitation and electricity is real, and the app shows it as two columns on the same row: what the regime does (its rows in the science library) and what it draws per tray. No "optimal", no suggested regime.
9. **The document's own study figures are comparison lines, never the facility's.** The building-integrated microgreens LCA the document leans on (its ref. 5: infrastructure about 11%, electricity about 54%, LED about 4.4 and seed about 4.04 kg CO2e per kg harvested) describes another system. Each is registered as a figure of that study and shown on the Inventory page as "the cited study's system" beside the facility's own computed share, so a reader sees whether the farm's profile resembles the literature's.
