# Phase 2 — Growing domain  status: IN PROGRESS (parts 1–8 done; part 9 all but the mixed trays; part 10 the pages done, the deep cut open)

The variety as the master record and the cost basis; the grow plan with seed, medium, nutrient and light lines replacing the crop plan; the stage schedule replacing thermal processes; tray formats and grow units replacing vessels; nutrition targets replacing crediting; produce-safety control points; the science library and the glossary in the app; Vallecito data seeded. `outline.md` §4 is the domain model, §5 the engine rules, `glossary.md` the naming authority, `science-library.md` the source register.

The library stores grow plans and the engine costs and sizes them on the grow model. The app runs on localhost against Neon with fourteen migrations applied and every page a subscriber or Rob would open first on the grow model. No Phase 1-era plan, menu or kitchen study remains in the app or its tests; no planning, scheduling or capacity path reads a rack. The production ledger costs a sowing by its cost card per tray, and a sowing record is one lot per variety in grams. The farm is set up two ways on Equipment: a home grow room (the seed) and a rented commercial facility (entered in a forecast, counted only once selected). What Phase 1-era material is left is the facility engine's kitchen rules (`todo.md`), the retired seed crews a saved forecast can still name (`LEGACY_SEED_CREWS` in `crews.ts`) and the supplier keywords (`todo.md`). Every module reads the grow plan itself: its cost card (`costPlan`), its purchase lines (`purchaseLines`) and the prices and measured figures the resolver attaches; no kitchen-era plan shape remains.

**How a session on this phase starts.** Read this file, then `docs/roadmap.md` and `docs/todo.md`. Run `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test` (68 files, 869 tests green). Start the app with `pnpm dev` from the repo root; `.env.local` carries the Neon string and `FARM_DEV_BYPASS_AUTH=1`, so `http://localhost:3000/farm` opens on the local admin with no sign-in. A migration runs with `pnpm db:migrate`; a change to the equipment or fixed-cost seed reaches the local workspace with `FARM_WORKSPACE=org_local_dev pnpm farm:sync-setup` (seed rows only; rows edited in the app are left alone). Commit at each completed step with a roadmap update (CLAUDE.md §6), only on Rob's approval; never push.

**Where the work stands.** The deep cut is done through step (6), and the rename through (R2): the database and the code say grow plan, the page is `/farm/grow-plans` and its former address redirects to `/farm`. Next is (R3), "input" as the old word for a line becomes line, then (R4), the vocabulary guard and the docs. No study has been approved on the local workspace yet; the first real approval is Rob's.
