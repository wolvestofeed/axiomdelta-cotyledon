## Phase definitions (Robert's framing, 2026-09-10 — use these consistently)

| Phase | Market | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | School market | Weekday mornings, roughly 180 days, Aug–May, hard AM peak, dark in summer |
| **2** | Corporate catering | Weekday midday, year-round, broader and more sophisticated menu |
| **3** | Ghost kitchen / consumer retail delivery | Evenings, weekends, and summer; made-to-order rhythm, delivery SLAs |

**The organizing principle:** the three markets have different models of operation, hours of
operation and workflow rhythms, and they funnel into ONE central facility. The phases are not ranked
by importance; they are staged to maximize utilization of a single heavy fixed-cost asset.

**Implication for the capacity model:** Phase 3 is what fills the evening trough and the summer dark
period that Phase 1 cannot cover. It is therefore the phase that makes facility economics work, not
the least important one. Any square-footage, equipment or staffing model must be built against the
combined load curve across all three, not against Phase 1 volume.

**Design discipline agreed:** design FROM the phase 3 end state, PRESENT from the phase 1 decisions
being made now. See the one-pager scope discussion in the Roadmap.
