## B. The business as it was run (from The Big Picture, PRS letter, Orders sheet)

- **Positioning:** "High Performance Microgreens" grown at altitude with Vallecito Creek water. Organic, heirloom, non-GMO. Mission framed around nutritional density over calories, food insecurity, local food resilience, heirloom seed protection.
- **Product forms sold:** live 1020 flats ($25 retail, $20 subscription, $16 wholesale/PRS), large trays (7x11) and small 5x5 inserts, cut microgreens (Tend listing: $8), pint-jar sprouts (~$4 target).
- **Customers on record (2023 Orders):** 6 private households (Durango, Vallecito), 2 restaurants (Yellow Carrot / Chef Sari; Meadow Market), plus Pine River Shares proposal (200 family units/month tier). Standing orders were 1–2 large trays per household per cycle.
- **Channels modeled in the 2023 forecast** (annual revenue scenarios, mostly aspirational): SJMA/SO $40; Pine River Shares $19,200; farmer's markets $12,293; online $22,693; CPG wholesale $16,195; restaurant ingredient wholesale $22,250. Total scenario $75,591/yr. Also listed: SW Farm Fresh Co-op, edibles companies.
- **Tend (farm software) sheet:** 12 crops entered with heirloom/non-GMO/organic flags, plus feature requests (sprouts category, mixed tray sizes, rack-based locations).

## E. Time study (1020 tray, one grow cycle)

Production 18 min (receive/sort 1, prep station 1, prep trays 1, sow 3, germ watering 1, blackout watering 1, light watering 3, nutrient prep 1, inspection/sanitize 5) + harvest 9 min (prep 1, knife harvest 5, weigh 1, pack/label 1, clean 1) = **27 min/flat**. Wage scenarios $18/$20/$22.
Note: for LIVE flats delivered uncut, the 9-min harvest block largely disappears.

Consumables allocated per flat: tray set $0.013 (OTG 3-pc set $13.33 amortized 1000 uses), medium $0.012, nutrients $0.014, sanitizer $0.05. Lighting: Mars Hydro $85 fixture amortized $0.048/flat; energy $0.08/flat/day at LPEA $0.1256/kWh (160 W shelf, 16 h).

## G. Facility layout scenario (layout sheet)

10x30 ft room, 3 rows × 6 racks, 5 tiers, 4 flats/shelf = **360 flats capacity**; 12-rack variant with 8x10 retail corner = 240 flats. Scenario math: 120 flats/mo at $16 = $1,920 rev, $300 COGS, $800 rent/util → $820 net for ~50 labor hrs ($16.40/hr). 240 flats at $20 = $4,800 rev, $600 COGS, $2,000 labor → $2,200 net. Retail corner notes: 8 ft counter, 18 sq ft shelving per level, 3x6 ft herb wall.
