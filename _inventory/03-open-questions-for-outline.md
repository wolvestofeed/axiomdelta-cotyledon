# Inventory 03 — Open questions to settle before the outline

Not answers. Things the Vallecito material does not resolve for Austin, grouped by phase.

## Phase 1 — Home-based live-flat subscription (South Austin)

1. Product unit: 1020 live flat vs 7x11 large tray vs 5x5 inserts. Vallecito households ordered large trays, not full 1020s. What is the Austin default SKU?
2. Cadence: bi-weekly vs monthly. A live flat is eaten over ~7–10 days; monthly implies 2 deliveries or a bigger unit.
3. Fulfillment: porch pickup at the house, a delivery route, or a central pickup point (gym, yoga studio, coffee shop). Tray return/deposit policy for live flats.
4. Pricing baseline: Vallecito was $25 retail / $20 sub / $16 wholesale with $9 labor per flat. Live uncut flats cut ~9 min of labor. Austin cost of seed shipping, electricity (Austin Energy rates), and summer cooling load are unknowns.
5. Texas rules: Texas Cottage Food Law does not cover fresh produce, but raw uncut produce sold direct by the grower is generally exempt from retail food permits. Sprouts (FSMA Subpart M) are the risk item. Needs a Texas / Travis County / Austin Public Health check before sprouts go on the menu.
6. Home grow capacity: how many racks fit in the South Austin house, and the cooling/humidity situation in a Texas summer.
7. Seed source: True Leaf still, or a Texas/regional supplier to cut freight.
8. Brand: new name for Austin, or keep "Vallecito" lineage as origin story. Domain.
9. Intake model: the "grow for your deficiencies" one-on-one consult. Is that a free onboarding call, a paid Ayurveda/nutrition consult, or an app form (FTW pattern)?
10. First customer pool: Austin men's group, neighbors, personal training clients, restaurants. Which first?

## Phase 2 — Massage school and licensure (Oct/Nov 2026 → Apr/May 2027)

11. Texas LMT requires a 500-hour program plus MBLEx and TDLR license. Which school, and does the 6-month timeline include the exam window.
12. Does bodywork start at the house / mobile before the commercial space, and under what insurance.
13. Scope of "energy work" and "movement therapy" offered under vs. alongside the LMT license (Reiki, qigong instruction, breathwork are unlicensed in Texas; anything touching the body under a massage claim is not).

## Phase 3 — Commercial flex space wellness center (target summer 2027)

14. Square footage split: grow room in back (Vallecito layout math: 10x30 ft = 360 flats), 1–2 treatment rooms, movement room, retail corner, reception. Rough target 1,500–2,500 sq ft?
15. Zoning: a grow operation in a flex/light-industrial bay plus a massage establishment (Austin requires a separate massage establishment license from TDLR) plus retail. Confirm all three can coexist in one lease.
16. Capital: Vallecito rack cost ~$1,058/rack; 18 racks ≈ $19k for grow build-out alone before HVAC, plumbing, treatment rooms, buildout, deposits.
17. Revenue mix goal: flats subscriptions vs bodywork sessions vs classes vs retail. Which one carries the rent.
18. Operating entity: one LLC for farm + wellness, or two, and does it sit under WTF Publishing / AxiomDelta.
19. Software: Rob's direction (2026-09-25) is to adapt Muse Kitchen · Impact OS into the microgreens and sprouts production system. Open sub-questions, see inventory 04 §F:
    a. Fork into this repo, run as a second tenant inside CompTable, or extract Impact OS as a generic core with two domain configs.
    b. Vocabulary swap scope: recipe → crop plan, portion → flat/tray/oz, vessel → shelf/rack, cook → sow, chill → blackout, meal-pattern crediting → nutrient matrix.
    c. What Muse lacks that Phase 1 needs on day one: recurring subscription billing, pickup/delivery routing, tray returns, per-customer nutrition targets.
    d. Whether the wellness-center side (bookings, classes, retail POS) lives in the same OS or stays on AxiomDelta.coach.
